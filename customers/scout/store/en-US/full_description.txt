Scout is the field app from Boon Agriculture for collecting farm data where the work happens: in the field, with or without a signal.

Open your farm projects from Scout Cloud, record soil samples, crop observations, pest and disease sightings, and photos on the map, then sync everything back when you are online again.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Work offline: download a project once and keep collecting data with no mobile signal.
• Record points, lines and areas on the map using your phone's GPS or an external GNSS receiver.
• Fill in simple forms with drop-down lists, dates, numbers, notes and photos.
• See your fields, sample points and earlier records on top of satellite or street maps.
• Sync to Scout Cloud: upload your changes and receive your team's updates.
• Pick up where you left off: Scout reopens your last project when you start it.

WHO IT IS FOR
Agronomists, field officers, farm managers and scouts who need reliable, location-based records from the field.

A Scout Cloud account is needed to sync projects. Contact Boon Agriculture to get access.
